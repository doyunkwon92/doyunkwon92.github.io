Doyun Kwon

MD · Department of Rehabilitation Medicine

Dedicated to advancing the frontiers of medical science. My research intersects clinical rehabilitation and cutting-edge technology, focusing on stroke recovery, neurostimulation, biomarker identification, AI applications in medicine, and neuroengineering.

Research Areas

Neurostimulation

Investigating non-invasive and invasive neuromodulation techniques to enhance neural plasticity and motor recovery following neurological injuries.

Stroke Rehabilitation

Developing comprehensive, evidence-based rehabilitation protocols to improve functional outcomes and quality of life for stroke survivors.

Biomarkers

Identifying reliable neuroimaging and molecular biomarkers for predicting recovery trajectories and personalizing rehabilitation strategies.

AI in Medicine

Leveraging machine learning algorithms to analyze complex clinical data, automate prognosis, and optimize treatment pathways in rehabilitation.
